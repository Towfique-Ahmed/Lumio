Welcome to Lumio Studio

Broadcast live to YouTube, Facebook or any RTMP server — straight from your browser. Pick your camera and microphone to enter the studio.

Lumio needs camera & mic permission. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Go Live.

Add every platform you want to broadcast to. Stream keys never leave your Lumio server.

Where do I find my stream key?

YouTube: YouTube Studio → Go live → Stream — copy the Stream key (Lumio uses the RTMPS ingest a.rtmps.youtube.com/live2).

Facebook: facebook.com/live/producer → Go live → Streaming software — copy the Stream key (ingest live-api-s.facebook.com:443/rtmp).

Custom: paste a full rtmp:// or rtmps:// URL including the key.
