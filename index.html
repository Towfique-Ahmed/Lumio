Everything a webinar page needs

Built on the official YouTube IFrame and Facebook Video Player embeds.

▶️

YouTube & Facebook Live

Works with YouTube videos, live streams, channel “always-live” links and any public Facebook Live video or fb.watch link.

⏳

Countdown lobby

Schedule your webinar and attendees who arrive early see a live countdown that flips to the stream automatically at start time.

💬

Live chat

YouTube live chat is embedded right beside the player. Facebook events link viewers to the conversation on Facebook.

🔗

One shareable link

Every webinar gets a self-contained link that carries the whole event — send it by email, Slack or socials.

🎤

Speaker spotlight

Add the host and speaker names, an agenda and a description so your page looks like an event, not just a video.

🔒

Private by design

Everything is stored in your browser. No servers, no tracking, no sign-up. Your data never leaves your device.

Live in three steps

1

Go live on your platform

Start a live stream on YouTube (via YouTube Studio) or Facebook (via Live Producer) as you normally would, and copy its URL.

2

Create your webinar

Paste the link into Lumio, add a title, schedule, speakers and description. Lumio detects the platform automatically.

3

Share the link

Send the generated webinar link to your audience. They get a countdown, then the live player and chat — on any device.
